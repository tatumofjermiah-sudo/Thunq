# Thunq

> *Can you return to control, again and again, over time?*

A human discipline system built on time, cycles, and repetition — structured through the **3–6–9 progression**.

## What It Is

Thunq is not a diet app. It is a **measurement system** for repeatable control.

It measures one thing:  
How many times can you return to a chosen action and complete it?

**Not intensity. Not extremes. Only: Completion → Repetition → Consistency.**

## The System

### A Cycle
A cycle is one completed fasting duration of a chosen level:
- 12 hours completed → 1 cycle
- 24 hours completed → 1 cycle
- 48 hours completed → 1 cycle

### Levels
| Duration | Trial |
|----------|-------|
| 12 hours | Entry |
| 24 hours | Standard |
| 48 hours | Extended |
| 72 hours | Deep |
| 144 hours | Mastery (6 days) |

### The 3–6–9 Progression

| Cycles | Rank |
|--------|------|
| 1–2 | Exposure (no rank) |
| 3 | **Third Gate** — First proof of repetition |
| 6 | **Sixth Path** — Stability forming |
| 9 | **Ninth Seal** — Behavior internalized |

### Identity
Users identify by completed cycles:
- *"Third Gate of 12"* — 3 completed 12-hour cycles
- *"Sixth Path of 24"* — 6 completed 24-hour cycles
- *"I hold the Ninth Seal of 48"* — 9 completed 48-hour cycles

## The Interface

Thunq features a **little person** — an animated SVG character that:
- Changes expression and posture based on your fasting state
- Visually reflects your rank through color, glow, and markings
- Breathes gently while you fast, celebrates when you complete a cycle
